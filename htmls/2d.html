Public Health Information - Kenya

Consequences of Missing Alt Text

1. Visually Impaired Users

Screen readers cannot describe images, making health information difficult to access.

2. Users with Slow Internet

If images fail to load, users may miss important health information.

3. Search Engines

Search engines have less information to understand and index the images.

4. Users with Limited Literacy

Users who rely on images to understand health instructions may struggle to understand the information.

Solution

Adding meaningful alt text improves accessibility and understanding.
