2(c) — Kenya Bank Portal: Hyperlinks

Kenya Bank Portal

1. Absolute Hyperlink

An absolute hyperlink uses the complete web address (URL) to link to another website. Example: Linking to the Central Bank of Kenya.

Visit Central Bank of Kenya

Use: To link to external websites such as regulators and partner organisations.

2. Relative Hyperlink

A relative hyperlink uses a file path to link to another page within the same website. Example: Linking to the bank's staff portal.

Open Staff Portal

Use: To navigate between internal pages such as staff, loans and customer support.

Difference

An absolute hyperlink uses a complete URL, while a relative hyperlink uses a path based on the current page's location.

The Staff Portal link is an example; a separate staff.html page would be needed for it to open a real page.

Consequences of Missing Alt Text

1. Visually Impaired Users

Screen readers cannot describe images, making health information difficult to access.

2. Users with Slow Internet

If images fail to load, users may miss important health information.

3. Search Engines

Search engines have less information to understand and index the images.

4. Users with Limited Literacy

Users who rely on images to understand health instructions may struggle to understand the information.

Solution

Adding meaningful alt text improves accessibility and understanding.
