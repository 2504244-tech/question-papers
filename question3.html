Climate Change

Climate change refers to long-term changes in temperatures and weather patterns. Human activities, especially burning fossil fuels, contribute to global warming.

In Kenya, climate change can contribute to droughts, floods and challenges in agriculture. Communities can help by planting trees, conserving water and reducing pollution.
